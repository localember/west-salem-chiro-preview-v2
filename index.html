Chiropractor in West Salem

West Salem Chiropractic is the Gonstead practice of Dr. Daniel Eoriatti, serving West Salem, Wisconsin and all of La Crosse County. If pain from a car accident, a spinal problem, or pregnancy has been hanging on, there is usually a mechanical reason for it. Dr. Eoriatti looks for that reason and builds a plan around what your exam shows, with a specific adjustment and practical advice you can use the rest of the week.

A specific, careful approach

Dr. Eoriatti specializes in the Gonstead technique. That means visualization, instrumentation, palpation, X-ray, and your symptoms are all considered before an adjustment. The same careful look applies when the problem is back pain, a disc injury, pain that travels into an arm or a leg, or the spinal and pelvic changes of pregnancy. You do not have to wait until the pain is unbearable to come in.

How are chiropractors qualified?

A Doctor of Chiropractic is a four-year doctoral program following undergraduate study, covering anatomy, physiology, diagnosis, imaging and hands-on technique, followed by national board examinations and state licensure. Licensed chiropractors also complete continuing education to stay licensed. In Wisconsin, that includes 40 hours of continuing education every two years.

When you come to West Salem Chiropractic, the first job is to understand what is going on and whether this office can help. If it can, we will lay out a plan that fits you.

Components of the Gonstead System

X-Rays

Full-spine films that show contour, posture, and an accurate count of the vertebrae.

Palpation

Hands-on static and motion checks for changes in contour, tone, texture, and movement.

Instrumentation

A dual-probe instrument compares temperature along the spine to help locate irritation.

Visualization

Posture and gait are observed from the moment you walk in, then checked against the other findings.

Case Management

Your symptoms are part of the picture, used to pinpoint the area that actually needs the adjustment.
